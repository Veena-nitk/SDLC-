Title: E-Commerce Multi-Vendor Retail & Inventory Architecture Standards
Jurisdiction: PCI-DSS Merchant Level 1 / Consumer Protection Regulations
Scope: Online Marketplace, Cart Checkout, Payment Gateway & Inventory Synchronization

Section 1: Inventory Reservation & Distributed Checkout
High-concurrency e-commerce systems must implement distributed ACID transaction locking or Saga patterns for real-time inventory deduction during shopping cart checkout. Concurrent order placement must prevent overselling or race conditions during flash sale events.

Section 2: Payment Tokenization & Merchant Segregation
Multi-vendor marketplaces must never process or store raw payment card data on web servers. Payment cards must be tokenized directly from customer browser using hosted payment fields. Multi-vendor payout splits must hold funds in escrow until shipment delivery confirmation.
